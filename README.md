## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, visa requirements, and weather tracking.

- [Visit Norway (Official Tourism Portal)](https://www.visitnorway.com/) - Official national tourist board portal featuring fjord itineraries, seasonal guides, and travel planning tools.
- [Norwegian Directorate of Immigration (UDI)](https://www.udi.no/en/) - Official government portal for Schengen visa entry rules, passport requirements, and visitor regulations.
- [Met.no (Norwegian Meteorological Institute)](https://www.yr.no/) - Official national weather service and forecasting platform providing precise meteorological data and aurora tracking.

## Transportation & Ferries

Navigating scenic railways, coastal express ships, car ferries, and domestic air travel.

- [Vy (Norwegian Railways)](https://www.vy.no/en) - Official national railway operator managing intercity trains and iconic scenic routes like the Bergen Line.
- [Hurtigruten Coastal Express](https://www.hurtigruten.com/) - Legendary coastal voyage and passenger line operating historic routes along the Norwegian fjord coastline.
- [Havila Voyages](https://www.havilavoyages.com/) - Coastal passenger shipping line offering eco-conscious travel along the Norwegian fjord route from Bergen to Kirkenes.
- [Entur National Journey Planner](https://entur.no/) - Comprehensive multimodal public transport planner combining all trains, buses, ferries, and local transit across Norway.
- [Widerøe Airlines](https://www.wideroe.no/en) - Regional airline operating the largest domestic network linking small coastal airports and northern communities.

## Destinations & Regions

### Oslo & Eastern Norway

- [Visit Oslo (Official City Portal)](https://www.visitoslo.com/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the Munch Museum, Oslo Opera House, fjord islands, and public transit.
- [Flåm Railway Official Site](https://www.visitflam.com/) - Visitor portal for booking the world-renowned steep railway journey descending from Myrdal to the Aurlandsfjord.

### Bergen & Fjord Norway

- [Visit Bergen](https://en.visitbergen.com/) - Official visitor guide for the historic Bryggen wharf, funicular railways, and gateway to the Hardangerfjord and Sognefjord.
- [Geirangerfjord Official Portal](https://www.fjordnorway.com/en/destinations/geirangerfjord) - Travel resources for exploring the UNESCO-listed dramatic deep-water fjord and waterfall viewpoints.

### Trondheim & Central Norway

- [Visit Trondheim](https://www.visittrondheim.no/en/) - Official tourist guide for Nidaros Cathedral, historic timber wharves, and culinary experiences in Central Norway.

### Lofoten & Northern Norway

- [Visit Lofoten](https://www.lofoten.info/) - Regional tourism hub for dramatic mountain peaks, fishing villages, surfing beaches, and midnight sun / northern lights viewing.
- [Visit Tromsø (Arctic Capital)](https://www.visittromso.no/) - Official visitor portal for Arctic expeditions, whale watching, dog sledding, and aurora borealis tours.

## Outdoor & Allemannsretten

Resources for exploring Norway's protected wilderness, hiking trails, and traditional right to roam.

- [The Right to Roam (Allemannsretten - Miljødirektoratet)](https://www.miljodirektoratet.no/) - Official government guide explaining public access rights for hiking, camping, and foraging in Norwegian nature.
- [DNT (Norwegian Trekking Association)](https://www.dnt.no/en/) - National association managing a vast network of marked hiking trails and backcountry mountain cabins across Norway.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency hotlines, mountain rescue, and road safety conditions.

- [Norwegian Red Cross Hjelp Korps](https://www.rodekors.no/) - Volunteer mountain rescue service providing emergency assistance in remote wilderness and alpine regions.
- [Statens Vegvesen (Norwegian Public Roads Administration)](https://www.vegvesen.no/en/) - Official portal for live road conditions, mountain pass closures, and scenic tourist route maps.
- [Emergency Numbers (Police 112 / Ambulance 113 / Fire 110)](https://www.politiet.no/) - Direct national emergency dispatch numbers operational across all municipalities.
